## Chatbot Creation Instructions

1. Click on **chatbots.empty.create** to go to the chatbot creation page.
2. Select the **Web** tab.
3. add the urls.
5. Click the "Build Now" button to start building your chatbot.

<img
  src="/docs/web1.png"
  className="w-[40rem] h-[25rem] mt-10 shadow-lg rounded-md"
/>


After uploading your files, the chatbot building process will analyze and create
a search index for future use. Once the chatbot is successfully built, you will
be redirected to the chatbot interaction page. Here, you can ask any questions
related to the uploaded files, such as 'Summarize the DPR paper.' Kouah AI's
chatbot leverages advanced retrieval methods and large language models (LLMs) to
deliver accurate responses to your queries.

<img
  src="/docs/file4.png"

/>

The built chatbot is saved on your dashboard. You can revisit it whenever you like.
