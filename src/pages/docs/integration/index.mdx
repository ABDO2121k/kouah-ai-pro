# **Integration Overview**

This page provides an overview of how to integrate the **Kouah AI** chatbot into your application and details regarding the `chatbotId`.

## **What is Chatbot ID?**

The **Chatbot ID** is a unique identifier for your specific chatbot instance. You will need this ID to successfully embed the chatbot in your application.

## **Where to Find Your Chatbot ID?**

You can obtain your **Chatbot ID** from two locations in the **Kouah AI dashboard**:

1. **Dashboard Home:**
   - Navigate to your **Kouah AI dashboard**.
   - Locate your chatbot listed there.
   - Copy the **Chatbot ID** directly from the displayed information.

<img
  src="/docs/card.png"
  className="w-[40rem] h-[25rem] mt-10 shadow-lg rounded-md"
/>

---

2. **Chatbot Settings:**
   - Go to **/dashboard/bot**.
   - Select the desired chatbot.
   - You can also copy the **Chatbot ID** from this section.

<img
  src="/docs/copy.png"
  className="w-[40rem] h-[25rem] mt-10 shadow-lg rounded-md"
/>
---

<Callout type="warning">
  **Confidentiality Notice:** The **Chatbot ID** should be kept confidential. If someone else gains access to your ID, they can use it to interact with your chatbot, potentially leading to misuse and unexpected billing.
</Callout>

## **Billing Information**

Your usage of the **Kouah AI** chatbot is subject to request limits based on your subscription plan. Please refer to your plan details for specific limits and pricing. 

### **Request Limits:**
- **Free Tier:** Limited to a specific number of requests per month.
- **Starter Tier:** Increased limits per month.
- **Standard Tier:** Further increased limits with additional features.
- **Business Tier:** Highest limits with premium features.
